The new Los Angeles areas for single-family homes

The formX team told us new single-family homes are rarely built in the Bay Area today, and asked to focus the single-family budget on Los Angeles: wealthy areas, high home values, a real chance of building a new house, and the fire areas. Our single-family ads in Meta and Google now target these 34 ZIP codes. Please mark each one: Approve, Discuss or Remove, then press Send at the bottom.
